Cheats & Codes

Every cheat needs two things: finish the story, then complete its own challenge. Tick the ones you’ve unlocked.

They unlock after you finish the story.

STEP 1

Finish the story

No cheat works until the main story is complete.
STEP 2

Do its challenge

Each cheat has its own task, like 100 workouts or 25 gold medals.
STEP 3

Open the phone

D-pad up for the phone, hold L1/LB, press R1/RB.
STEP 4

Enter the code

Tap the D-pad arrows in order. PS5 and Xbox Series.

Codes appear in several outlets and some are still disputed. Cheats with a missing code are known, but nobody has confirmed the input yet. Sources: VICE, Dexerto, Destructoid, The Loadout, G2A. Info as of Nov 22, 2026.

How to Play

A starter route for your first night in Vice City.

Meet Lucia & Jason

Story missions put you in the shoes of two leads, a Bonnie-and-Clyde style duo. Outside the story you’re free to swap between them.

Learn the controls early

Spend ten minutes in the settings: aiming assist, camera and button layout. Comfortable controls beat any upgrade.

Follow the story, wander often

Do a mission, then break away. Side activities and random encounters are where open-world games shine.

Earn, spend, repeat

Cash buys gear, vehicles and safehouses. Save before big purchases and never carry your whole fortune into a firefight.

Save often

Use safehouses and manual saves before risky missions. Future you will say thanks.
